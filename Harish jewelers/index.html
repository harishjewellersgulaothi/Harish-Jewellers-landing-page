Common questions

Is your gold hallmarked?

Yes. Our gold jewellery carries a BIS hallmark, and the details are on your bill.

Can I exchange or sell my old jewellery?

Yes. Bring your old pieces and we will test the purity in front of you and give you a written value.

Can you make a custom design?

Yes. Share a photo or an idea, and we will give you a design, weight estimate and price before we start.

How do I know today's gold price?

Message us on WhatsApp and we will send the current rate along with your quote.

Do I need an appointment to visit?

No, you are welcome any time during store hours. An appointment makes sure an advisor is free to give you their full time.
